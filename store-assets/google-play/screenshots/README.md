# Screenshot Source Draft

This directory is for future Google Play screenshots. This phase does not include final PNG or JPEG screenshot files.

## Language strategy

- English screenshots are the default store set.
- `zh-CN` screenshots are optional localized assets later.
- Do not mix languages within one listing screenshot set.
- Do not add marketing overlays unless they are localized consistently.
- Avoid time-sensitive words such as "new", "latest", "limited", "discount", and "free".

## Capture hygiene

- Use a clean emulator or clean device.
- Hide notifications and private status-bar content.
- Do not expose private IPs.
- Do not expose real Wi-Fi SSIDs.
- Do not expose phone numbers.
- Do not expose tokens or pairing secrets.
- Use test device names and neutral placeholder values.
- Do not show unrelated background apps.
- Do not include Play Store badges, install/download calls to action, or ranking claims.

## Expected screenshot sets

```text
screenshots/en-US/
screenshots/zh-CN/
```

The `en-US` set is the primary screenshot set. The `zh-CN` set should be prepared only if a localized Simplified Chinese Play listing is prepared later.
