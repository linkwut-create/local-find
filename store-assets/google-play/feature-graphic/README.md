# Feature Graphic Source Draft

This directory is for source notes and future output files for the Google Play feature graphic. Do not commit generated drafts unless they are intentionally prepared for review.

## Target output filename

`local-find-feature-1024x500.png`

## Target size

1024x500

## Recommended format

24-bit PNG, no alpha

## Concept

Local-first phone finder with a phone plus local network signal motif.

The graphic should communicate that Local Find helps a user find a nearby Android phone on the same local network. It should not imply cloud tracking, remote recovery, anti-theft protection, or Play Store endorsement.

## Suggested headline

Find your phone on your local network

## Visual constraints

- No Google Play badge.
- No Android robot unless license-safe.
- No ranking claims.
- No price, discount, or download claims.
- No exaggerated privacy or security claims.
- No "no tracking" claim unless precisely supported by the privacy policy.
- Keep key text and icon near center.
- Avoid tiny details.
- Avoid real IP addresses, tokens, QR contents, or device owner names.
